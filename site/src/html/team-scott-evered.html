Senior Project Manager

Scott Evered

After spending 16 years as a builder, working mainly on high-end residential builds in Hamilton, in 2017 I made the transition to Project Manager.

This is a role that I am extremely passionate about, and where I can put the skills I have acquired over the years to good use. One of my superpowers is attention to detail, so I put that to good use to ensure that the quality of the homes we build is exceptional. I really enjoy the challenge of high-end builds that are a little different, working alongside like-minded people. It is also awesome to work with clients and be responsible for bringing their dream home to life.

I live in Omokoroa and enjoy fishing, surfing, and spending time with my wife and three young children.
