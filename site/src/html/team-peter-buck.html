Director

Peter Buck

My early career began in finance roles in Sydney and London.

After returning to NZ with my partner Lisa, we co-founded Thorne Group alongside Gavin and my brother-in-law, Aaron.

I am proud of the business we’ve built, and even more proud of the ethical, values-driven way we go about it.

My focus is on Thorne Group’s finance and governance, along with structuring and supporting development collaborations. I’m driven to ensure every stakeholder has a positive experience with Thorne Group and that we continue to challenge the status quo in how we operate and deliver projects.

In my spare time, I love being outdoors with the family, particularly skiing, hiking in the backcountry and anything on the water.
