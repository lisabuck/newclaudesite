1. Assess

Love the location, but not the home?

You may love where you live but need more space, a better layout or a home that works better for you now.

We can help you assess whether renovating, extending or rebuilding will deliver the most practical and cost-effective outcome. Our advice considers the existing home, the site, council and Building Code requirements, likely construction costs and what you want to achieve.

Start with the existing property

Whether you already own the home or are considering purchasing it, we begin with a no-obligation property and site assessment.

  • The existing structure and layout
  • The age and condition of the home
  • What can realistically be retained
  • Potential structural work
  • Planning and compliance requirements
  • Site access, terrain, hazards and zoning
  • The feasibility of extending
  • The likely level of investment

The aim is to establish whether the home can be renovated or extended successfully without unnecessary cost or compromise.

Shape a brief and budget

We look at what you want to achieve and how well the existing property can support it.

  • What is worth retaining
  • Where alterations or extensions are needed
  • Whether the existing layout can be improved
  • Likely structural or compliance work
  • A realistic budget range
  • Whether rebuilding should also be considered
  • A clear brief for the architectural designer

This provides stronger direction before significant money is committed to design.

Renovate or rebuild?

Renovation

Renovation can be the right choice when the existing structure and layout support what you want to achieve.

It allows you to retain the parts of the home you value while improving its appearance, performance and functionality.

However, renovation carries uncertainty. Once walls, floors or ceilings are opened, hidden issues can emerge. Structures may require strengthening, services may need replacing and older work may not meet current requirements.

New build

A new build usually provides greater cost certainty and removes the limitations of the existing structure.

For major renovations, the construction timeframe may also be more similar than expected.

Our role is to give you a realistic comparison of the costs, risks and compromises so you can make an informed decision.

Managing the unknowns

Some renovation conditions cannot be confirmed until construction begins.
