Renovate, extend or start again?

Architectural Homes|Renovations|Development Collaborations

Choose a pathway that is right for you. Twenty years in the construction industry has given us the experience and insight to help you weigh up the options.

Love the location, but not the home?

Need more space than you currently have? Adding an extension, opening up living areas, or reworking the layout for better day-to-day use. Or do you start again, knock down and rebuild on the same site, giving yourself a fresh start in the same location with a home designed around your needs from the ground up.

Does it make sense?

The real question to ask is often not whether a renovation can be done, but rather if it makes sense. Early investigation, getting an unbiased assessment from someone with the right expertise and experience to assess whether the existing home is genuinely worth preserving, before committing to a particular pathway.

What will the Council require?

Often it is not just about what you want to do, but what the Council will require you to do. What begins as a straightforward renovation can become more complicated once consenting and compliance realities are factored in. Major renovations are seldom straightforward and generally demand the same level of planning and due diligence as a new build.

The certainty of starting again.

New builds are easier to price than renovations, as there are fewer unknowns and hidden factors to allow for. A knock-down rebuild on your current site offers a level of certainty that renovations cannot, and often protects your equity better by ensuring the finished product is a 100% modern asset without the risk of over-capitalising on a compromised structure.

Twenty years of weighing it up.

After more than 20 years building in the Bay, experience isn’t about knowing how to build, it’s knowing how to prevent the problems most people never see.
