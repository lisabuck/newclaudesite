Scope

Decisions made at the outset often have the greatest influence on the success of your project. With a strong network of trusted architects and designers, quantity surveyors, geotech consultants and engineers we offer more than just building.

Our team can provide the practical guidance and construction experience needed to help you evaluate options and make informed decisions, defining your brief before significant time or money is committed.

Design

Choosing the right team is one of the most important decisions you will make for your project, ensuring design, budget and buildability evolve together, not in isolation.

We work in partnership with your architect or designer or, with relationships developed over more than 20 years in the industry, help match you with a designer with the best fit for your project.

Early collaboration sets your project up for success with a team focussed on delivering a home that is beautifully designed, functional and cost-effective.

Pricing

Realistic pricing combined with competitive trade rates and discounts ensure you receive the best value for money.

We take an open, no-surprises approach to pricing, providing comprehensive and detailed cost estimates. Including price checks at key stages in the design process enables you to make informed design decisions and for adjustments to be made as the design evolves.

Project Management

Long before work begins on site, a dedicated Project Manager coordinates every aspect of your project – coordinating suppliers, scheduling trades, planning and sequencing construction and ensuring documentation is carefully managed.

Utilising cloud-based project management software to keep you up to date allows you to access and track progress from anywhere in the world.

Strict financial controls ensure your investment is protected with ongoing tracking and regular reporting against budget.

Build

Each of our projects is led by a foreman with more than 20 years of experience, supported by a small, consistent team of builders that remain accountable throughout.

Onsite systems are designed to reduce inefficiencies, improve coordination and ensure labour, materials and time are used where they add the greatest value.
