Qualified Carpenter

Denton McCarty

BCITO Qualified Carpenter.

Denton has been part of the Thorne Group team for over 10 years, starting with us as an apprentice and going from strength to strength ever since. He’s an incredibly valued member of our crew, known for a standout work ethic and an eye for detail that clients regularly comment on.

Over the years, Denton has worked on projects with extremely complex engineering, bringing a steady, practical approach on site and taking real pride in delivering quality workmanship. He works closely with the wider team to keep projects running smoothly, with a strong focus on safety, compliance, and doing things properly.

Outside of work, Denton’s all about family time. You’ll usually find him and his partner out adventuring with the kids: fishing, hitting the skate parks, heading to the beach, or getting out on a trail walk.
