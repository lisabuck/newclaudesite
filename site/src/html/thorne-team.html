Our journey

Two decades of building together

From our start as a small team building coastal homes in the Bay of Plenty, to a recognised name in architectural construction, we have been trusted with some of the region’s most complex sites. Our story is one of steady growth, building a team, constantly evolving craftsmanship and developing systems.

01
2006
Three skillsets combined

Aaron brought expertise in geotechnically challenging environments. Gavin returned from Europe with high-end building knowledge. Pete contributed finance and governance rigour.

02

400+ homes at Urban Ridge

Over 18 years, homes for people who wanted to downsize but not downspec. The same precision applied to compact homes as to our largest projects. Word of mouth did the work across the Bay.
